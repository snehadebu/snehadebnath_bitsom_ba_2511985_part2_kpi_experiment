# Recommendation Memo

## Executive Summary

The experiment evaluated a new onboarding campaign against the existing onboarding experience. The objective was to improve user activation, engagement, and paid conversion.

## North Star Metric

Paid Conversion Rate

This metric directly measures the percentage of users who convert to a paid subscription and is closely tied to business growth and revenue generation.

## KPI Tree Summary

The KPI tree focused on three major drivers:

1. Landing Page Performance
2. Onboarding Effectiveness
3. Trial Quality

Guardrail metrics included:

- Refund Rate
- Support Ticket Rate
- Average Revenue Per User

## Experiment Results

| Metric | Control | Treatment |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Paid Conversion Rate | 3.17% | 6.99% |
| Average Engagement Score | 57.03 | 62.93 |
| Average Days To Convert | 8.86 | 6.40 |
| Refund Rate | 0.00% | 0.42% |
| Support Ticket Rate | 14.72% | 24.76% |

The treatment group significantly outperformed the control group in conversion and engagement metrics.

## Hypothesis Test Interpretation

Control conversion rate was 3.17% while treatment conversion rate was 6.99%.

The treatment delivered an absolute improvement of 3.82 percentage points and a relative lift of approximately 120%.

The results support rejecting the null hypothesis and indicate that the treatment improves paid conversion.

## Guardrail Analysis

Positive findings:

- Higher engagement scores
- Faster conversion times
- Higher conversion rates

Risks identified:

- Refund rate increased slightly
- Support ticket rate increased noticeably

These guardrail metrics should continue to be monitored after rollout.

## Segment Insights

### Region Analysis

Treatment outperformed control in all regions.

### Device Analysis

The strongest gains were observed among mobile users.

### Traffic Source Analysis

Organic, Paid Search, and Referral traffic showed strong improvement.

Social traffic performed slightly worse than the control group and should be monitored.

## Final Recommendation

Launch the new onboarding campaign to all users.

The treatment produced substantially higher conversion performance while maintaining acceptable guardrail behavior.

## Risks and Limitations

- Social traffic users showed lower conversion performance.
- Support ticket rates increased.
- The experiment covered a limited testing period.

## Next Steps

1. Roll out the treatment experience.
2. Monitor refund and support ticket rates.
3. Investigate social traffic performance.
4. Continue tracking conversion and engagement metrics.
