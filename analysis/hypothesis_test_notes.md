Hypothesis Test
Business Question

Should the new onboarding campaign be launched to all users?

Null Hypothesis (H0)

The treatment onboarding campaign does not improve paid conversion rate compared with the control onboarding experience.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1)

The treatment onboarding campaign improves paid conversion rate compared with the control onboarding experience.

Test Type

One-tailed test.

Significance Level

0.05

Primary Metric

Paid Conversion Rate

Results
Control Users: 693
Treatment Users: 715
Control Conversions: 22
Treatment Conversions: 50
Control Conversion Rate: 3.17%
Treatment Conversion Rate: 6.99%
Absolute Improvement: 3.82 percentage points
Relative Lift: 120.5%
Interpretation

The treatment group achieved a substantially higher paid conversion rate than the control group. The treatment generated approximately 120% higher conversion performance, indicating that the new onboarding experience positively impacts user activation and paid conversion.

Decision

Reject the null hypothesis and support the treatment rollout recommendation.
